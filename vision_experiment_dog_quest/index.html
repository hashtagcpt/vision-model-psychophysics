Vision Science: ON vs. OFF Difference of Gaussians (DoG)

Welcome to this psychophysics experiment. In this task, you will discriminate between ON-center and OFF-center Difference of Gaussians (DoG) stimuli across retinal eccentricities.

Instructions:

  • Keep your eyes focused on the single stationary central fixation cross throughout each trial.
  • A target will briefly flash for 120 milliseconds in your peripheral vision (at 2°, 4°, 6°, or 8° eccentricity to the left or right of fixation).
  • Identify the polarity of the stimulus:
    • ON-center: Bright center with dark surround.
    • OFF-center: Dark center with bright surround.
  • Respond using your keyboard:
    • ON-center (Bright center): Press F or ↑ (Up Arrow)
    • OFF-center (Dark center): Press J or ↓ (Down Arrow)
  • Trial Feedback: The central fixation cross will briefly turn green (pleasant chime) for correct responses, or red (buzz) for incorrect responses.
  • Stimulus contrast is adaptively adjusted across 12 interleaved conditions using the Bayesian QUEST+ algorithm (Watson, 2017) to find your contrast threshold.
Screen & Viewing Distance: 57.3 cm (41.9 px/°)
